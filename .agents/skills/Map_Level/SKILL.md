## Map Design and Progressive Difficulty Scaling Rules
 - Principle of Gradual Difficulty Escalation: All map chunks across every phase must be designed so that the difficulty finely and progressively scales up from earlier to later segments.

 - Phase 1 (Quantitative Scaling Focus): Induce a smooth difficulty curve on relatively flat terrain by densely packing SpikeTraps or increasing the spawn density of basic monsters (Base, Bird). (Introductory control phase)

 - Phase 2 (Mechanical Execution Focus): Test the player's precision control through complex MovingPlatform patterns (Horizontal ➡️ Diagonal ➡️ Vertical), inertia management, and landing controls utilizing SlowPads.

 - Phase 3 (Complex Gimmick Synergies): Actively utilize combinations of mechanics rather than standalone gimmicks. (e.g., Timed spike evasion while on a slow pad, fighting a Bird on a moving platform, tentacle spawns at landing zones, etc.)

 - Strict Exclusion Zone Compliance: When spawning chests or monsters, restricted areas—such as the margin around the player's spawn point or directly on top of traps/jump pads—must be strictly excluded.
